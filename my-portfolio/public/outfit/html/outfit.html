The brief

Most people already own a considered wardrobe — the daily friction is composition, not acquisition. Outfit treats a closet as structured data and turns temperature, mood and occasion into styling constraints, so getting dressed becomes a synthesis problem an engine can solve rather than a decision you re-make every morning. The system spans eight core screens: a landing experience that sells the idea, an onboarding questionnaire that calibrates taste, a style-blend engine, the wardrobe index, the outfit generator itself, and account access.
